Helping international students in Denmark

Exchange or not, your international adventure is better with ESN!

Coming to Denmark for an exchange, studies or internship? Fantastisk!
We are in 6 locations in Denmark, where our local student volunteers are to help you getting the best experience. We organise cultural events, social meetups, trips, parties and much more. All for you to integrate, know little bit about Denmark and simply have fun. Check our local sections pages for current activities.
